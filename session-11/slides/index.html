Session 11 · why tonight matters

The question every AI product hits

“Who approved this refund, and can you prove it?” — every auditor who reads an AI system's payment log
The principle
Maker-checker
Banks require a second person before money moves: one prepares, another approves, and both leave a trail. An agent that can pay refunds needs the same control, and the approval has to survive until someone gives it.
In production
Servers restart
A deploy, a crash, a closed laptop. A manager answers on Monday a question the system asked on Friday. If the run lived only in memory, the question is gone; if the payment ran twice, the customer is paid twice.
Tonight, live
Four tickets, four gaps
v7 on four ordinary tickets: it can't pause for an approval, forgets the conversation, has never heard of a returning customer, and answers “and for the watch?” from the wrong passages.

What you'll learn tonight

  1. SAVEDA checkpointer on disk: threads, get_state, and the full history of a run.
  2. PAUSEinterrupt() and Command(resume=), and the four rules. The node re-runs from the top; you'll watch a print fire twice.
  3. SHAPESApprove, edit, reject; review a tool call; validate the person's answer.
  4. MONEYCode proposes, a manager decides, a separate node pays, and why who may resume is checked before the graph runs.
  5. DURABLEA crash after the payment: invoke(None), idempotency, durability modes. Then time travel: fork a bad run at the step that went wrong.
  6. MEMORYSummaries with RemoveMessage; the Store; the namespace as an authorization boundary; identity from context, never from the chat.
  7. MEANINGMemories found by meaning, and v8's recall and remember nodes.
  8. FOLLOW-UPSConversational query rewriting, measured; and the typed event stream every client reads.

interrupt(): the four rules

RuleBreaks howDo this
1. On resume, the node re-runs from the topanything above interrupt() happens again: a payment pays twicepause early; effects after it, in another node, idempotent
2. No broad try/except around itthe pause is swallowed; the run finishes without asking anyonecatch narrowly, or re-raise GraphInterrupt
3. Same interrupts, same orderanswers are matched by position and land on the wrong questionask only about things saved in the state
4. Plain JSON payloadsthe form can't be saved, or sent to a browserdicts of strings, numbers, lists
