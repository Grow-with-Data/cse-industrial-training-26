# Gold-tier courtesy rules

1. **Gold members get free return shipping** (returns-policy 3.2). Say so whenever a return is on the
   table, before they have to ask.
2. **Gold tier does not include free delivery.** Standard delivery is free for everyone on orders of $75 or
   more; below that, gold members pay it too. If they ask, say it plainly and without apology.
3. **Thank them for being a member once, at most.** Not in every reply.
4. **Never invent a perk.** If they ask about one that no policy mentions, say a member of the team will
   confirm it.
