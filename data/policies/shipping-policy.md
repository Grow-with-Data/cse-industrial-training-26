# ShopWise Shipping Policy

*Applies to all orders placed on the ShopWise online store. Last updated 1 June 2026.*

## 1. Where we ship

1.1 ShopWise ships to addresses within the **United States only**, including PO boxes.

1.2 We do **not** ship internationally, and we cannot forward orders to freight-forwarding services.

## 2. Delivery options and rates

| Option | Delivery time (business days) | Price | Free when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 | $4.99 | order subtotal is **$75 or more** |
| Express | 1–2 | $12.99 | never |

2.1 Delivery times are counted from dispatch, not from the order date. Orders placed before 2 pm
Eastern on a business day are dispatched the same day; later orders are dispatched the next business
day.

2.2 Express is not available to PO boxes.

## 3. Tracking

3.1 A carrier tracking number is emailed **within 24 hours of dispatch**, and appears in your order
history at the same time.

3.2 Tracking can show "label created" for up to 24 hours before the carrier first scans the parcel.
This is normal and is not a delay.

## 4. Delays and lost parcels

4.1 If we cannot ship within the time stated at checkout, we will tell you before the original date
passes, give you a revised date, and offer you the choice of waiting or cancelling for a full refund.

4.2 A parcel is treated as lost if tracking has not updated for **7 business days**. Contact support
and we will send a replacement or refund the order, at your choice.
