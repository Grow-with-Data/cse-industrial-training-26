# ShopWise Returns & Refunds Policy

*Applies to all orders placed on the ShopWise online store. Last updated 1 June 2026.*

## 1. The return window

1.1 You may return most items within **30 days of the delivery date** shown in your order history.
The window is counted from delivery, not from the order date.

1.2 **Opened audio products** — headphones, earbuds and speakers whose seal has been broken — may be
returned within **15 days of delivery**, for hygiene reasons. Unopened audio products follow the
30-day window in 1.1.

1.3 Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories included.

## 2. What cannot be returned

2.1 Gift cards and store credit, including gift cards received as a present.

2.2 Items bought in a clearance sale and marked "final sale" on the product page.

2.3 Items damaged by misuse or accident. Faults that appear in normal use are handled under the
Warranty Policy, not this policy.

## 3. How refunds are paid

3.1 Approved refunds are paid to the **original payment method** within **5–7 business days** of the
returned item reaching our warehouse.

3.2 Return shipping is paid by the customer, except for **gold-tier members**, who receive free return
shipping, and for items that arrived faulty or were sent in error.

3.3 A support assistant cannot approve a return or a refund. A member of the returns team reviews
every request and confirms the outcome by email.

## 4. Starting a return

4.1 Reply to your order confirmation email, or write to support, with your order number and the
reason for the return. The returns team replies within one working day with a return label and
instructions.

4.2 Pack the item securely. Items damaged in return transit because of poor packaging may be refused.
