# ShopWise Warranty Policy

*Applies to products sold by ShopWise. Last updated 1 June 2026.*

## 1. What the warranty covers

1.1 The ShopWise warranty covers **faults in materials or workmanship that appear in normal use**.
It is a limited warranty and runs from the **delivery date**.

1.2 It does not cover accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens), misuse, normal wear,
cosmetic marks, or damage from unauthorised repair.

## 2. How long each product type is covered

| Product type | Examples | Warranty period |
|---|---|---|
| Audio | headphones, earbuds, speakers | **12 months** |
| Wearables | smartwatches, fitness bands | **24 months** |
| Home | kettles, small kitchen appliances | **6 months** |
| Outdoor | backpacks, bottles, camping gear | **no warranty** |

2.1 A fault that first appears after the warranty period has ended is not covered, even if the
product has been used very little.

2.2 Outdoor products carry no ShopWise warranty. Faults that appear within 30 days of delivery can
still be raised under the Returns & Refunds Policy.

## 3. Making a claim

3.1 Write to support with your **order number** and a **photo or short video** that shows the fault.

3.2 If the product is **in stock**, we send a **replacement first**. If it is **out of stock**, we
**refund** the price paid instead.

3.3 We pay the shipping for warranty replacements in both directions.

3.4 A support assistant cannot approve a claim. The warranty team reviews every claim and replies
within two working days.

## 4. Your rights under the law

4.1 This is a **written warranty** on a consumer product, and it is designated a **limited warranty**,
as the federal Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act requires every written warranty to be designated "full" or
"limited". Its terms are shown on every product page before purchase.

4.2 This warranty is in addition to any rights you have under federal or state law, and does not
limit them.
